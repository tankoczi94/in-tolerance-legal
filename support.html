Frequently asked questions

  • How do I delete a log entry? Open the entry and choose delete — changes apply immediately.
  • How do I export my data? Settings → "Export my data" produces a JSON file with everything linked to your account.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → "Delete account", then confirm. This permanently erases your account and data and cannot be undone.
  • How do I restore a Pro purchase? Profile → "Restore purchases". If it doesn't unlock Pro, make sure you're signed in to the same Apple/Google account used to buy the subscription.
  • How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple/Google, not in the app — iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
  • I found a bug or something isn't working. Email us at the address above with your account email and a description of what happened — screenshots help.
