in-tolerance connector for Claude

The in-tolerance connector lets you ask Claude about the food, symptom and sleep entries you have logged in the in-tolerance app, and ask Claude to add new entries for you. It works on your own account only.

in-tolerance shows patterns in your own logs. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose allergies or intolerances. Talk to a clinician about medical questions.

Before you connect

  • An in-tolerance account (sign up in the iOS or Android app).
  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Some tools need an in-tolerance Pro subscription; see the table below.

Connect

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and add in-tolerance.
  2. Sign in to your in-tolerance account on the consent page (email or Google).
  3. Read the consent text, confirm you are 18+, tick the health-data consent box and approve.
  4. Claude can now use the tools below. Try: “What did I eat yesterday?”

Server URL (if Claude asks for it): https://mcp.in-tolerance.app/mcp

Free accounts can use the connector with a shorter history window. Entries Claude creates are labelled “via Assistant” in the app.

Your data and control

  • Claude only receives what a request needs, and only after you consent.
  • Anthropic handles data it receives under its own privacy policy. See our Privacy Policy for details.
  • Disconnect at any time in the app under Profile → Connected apps, or remove the connector in Claude. Disconnecting stops further access.
  • To delete entries or your account, see Support.

Troubleshooting

  • “Couldn’t reach the MCP server” or sign-in fails: remove the connector and add it again.
  • A tool is missing: child tools appear only when your account has a child; describe_food and get_insights need Pro.
  • Still stuck: email support@in-tolerance.app with your account email and what happened.
